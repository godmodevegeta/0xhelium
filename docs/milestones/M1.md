# M1 — Deterministic shove and authoritative fall detection (CPU)

Status: **implemented and measured; awaiting review.** The Go1 walks normally in a scene where its
trunk, hips, thighs and knees can hit the floor, and its nominal trajectory is unchanged from M0. A
100 N sideways shove for 0.1 s makes it fall: tilt passes 45° at t = 2.504 s, 0.504 s after the
shove starts.

Spec: `.kiro/specs/fall-detection/`. ADR: `docs/decision-log/0002-m1-fall-contact-scene.md`.
Branch `m1-fall-detection`, based on tag `m0-baseline` (`cfe4b13`). Single writer: no other commits
landed during the work.

gait phase; this is not a survival rate and not a threshold. The deterministic runner has no seeds
to vary. M2 adds paired multi-condition runs.

Left and right differ, most likely because the stance feet at t = 2.0 s are not symmetric. For

(speed, random direction, 60° tilt rule), so the numbers are not directly comparable.

## How it works

### External force

`MjData.xfrc_applied[body]` holds a world-frame force and torque applied at that body's centre of
mass during `mj_step`. It persists until overwritten. The runner now steps the 5 physics substeps
of each Tick one at a time. Before each substep it zeros the whole array and, if the 0-based
substep index i satisfies 500 ≤ i < 525, writes the world force into the trunk row. Reset uses
`mj_resetData`, which also zeros it. The heading-frame direction (+x forward at reset, +y left, +z
up) is rotated about world z by the trunk's yaw at reset (0 here), so world = heading frame in
these runs. Start and duration must be exact multiples of the 0.004 s substep, so the window is
exact rather than rounded. The per-Tick impulse is recorded in `trajectory.json` and sums to
10.0 N·s.

### Contact geometry and the scene

A geom pair is tested for collision only if `contype₁ & conaffinity₂` or `contype₂ & conaffinity₁`
is non-zero. In `walk.xml` the floor is `contype=1, conaffinity=0`, the foot spheres
`conaffinity=1`, and everything else `0/0`, so only feet touch the floor. The `fall-contact` scene
is built in memory with `MjSpec` from the pinned XML and sets `conaffinity=1` on 29 non-foot
collision geoms: the trunk box and 2 cylinders, 10 hip cylinders, 12 thigh capsules and 4 upper-shin
capsules (`calf1`). Robot `contype` stays 0, so the robot cannot collide with itself. The pinned
file is untouched (asset lock and verify-assets unchanged).

The lower-shin capsule just above each foot (`calf2`, 4 geoms) stays non-colliding. Measured at
the M0 code before writing M1:

| Variant (10 s, command) | Non-foot floor contacts during normal walking | max \|qpos − M0\| |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all 33 non-foot geoms solid, (1, 0, 0) | `RR_calf` lower capsule, 5 substeps | 2.0e-6 m |
| all 33 non-foot geoms solid, (1.5, 0, 0) | lower capsule of all 4 calves, 43 substeps, ≤ 7.1 mm deep | **0.41 m** |
| `calf2` excluded (shipped), 29 commands incl. the 27-point grid | none | **0** |

The policy was trained in the feet-only scene, where the lower shin dips through the floor at
speed. Making it solid changed the gait, and counting it as a fall would have called normal fast
walking a fall.

### Fall detection

`FallMonitor.check` runs after every `mj_step` (250 Hz) and stores the first occurrence of each
reason:

| Reason | Kind | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nonfinite_state` | numerical | any NaN/inf in qpos, qvel or qacc |
| `mujoco_warning` | numerical | any MuJoCo warning counter non-zero |
| `nonfoot_contact` | physical | contact between the floor and one of the 29 fall geoms with `dist ≤ 0` |
| `tilt` | physical | trunk `up_z = 1 − 2(qx² + qy²) < cos 45°` |
| `low_height` | physical | trunk `qpos[2] < 0.18 m` |

The primary reason is the earliest event; ties go in the table order. `kind` is `physical` or
`numerical` by the primary reason, and `numerical_failure` is reported separately whenever any
numerical event occurs, so a NaN never reads as a fall. After a numerical event the episode stops
(`truncated: true`). After a physical fall it continues to the horizon for replay, but distance,
speed and touchdown metrics stop at the last Tick before the fall. Contact events are stamped one
substep earlier than state events, because `data.contact` after `mj_step` describes the state at
the start of that step. The 45° and 0.18 m thresholds are the existing `WalkCriteria` values, and
M0's per-Tick `upright_checks` now uses the same predicate functions. Foot-floor contact is never a
fall; the four per-foot flags are recorded as in M0.

## Limitations

- **Lower shins and feet do not count as fall contact.** A fall that lands only on them is caught
  by tilt or height.
- **Post-fall physics is outside the training distribution.** The policy never felt trunk-floor
  contact during training; behaviour after a fall is shown for replay only.
- **One episode per condition.** The runner is deterministic, so repeating a run gives the same
  result; it says nothing about other gait phases or start times. The shove table above is
  exploratory, not a survival curve.
- **The push is idealized:** a constant force at the trunk COM with zero torque, switched on and off
  instantly. It is not a contact with an object.
- **Contact timing resolution is one substep** (4 ms), and contact events refer to the state at the
  start of the step that detected them.
- The fall thresholds (45°, 0.18 m) are project defaults, not physical truths.
- Identical results are claimed only within one build and machine.
- Inherited from M0: trained in MJX Warp, evaluated in C MuJoCo; the policy JSON license is assumed
  to be the weeble repository MIT license.
